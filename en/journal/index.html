SAIGON NEEDLE JOURNAL

What is Irezumi?

Irezumi is more than a motif or visual style. It is an approach to building a composition around the body, where subject, background, placement and time work together.

02

SAIGON NEEDLE JOURNAL

Designing for the body

A successful large-scale tattoo is not a flat illustration transferred to skin. The drawing must respond to muscle, joints, movement and the way the body is seen from different angles.

03

SAIGON NEEDLE JOURNAL

Tebori and machine

Machine and tebori can serve the same composition in different ways. The choice of tool follows the desired texture, rhythm and working process rather than becoming the subject itself.
